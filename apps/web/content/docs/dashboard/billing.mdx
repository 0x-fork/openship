<Callout title="Openship Cloud resources">
Billing manages the selected Cloud server. Your own servers are free to manage;
linked self-hosted installations can also manage Cloud billing.
</Callout>

## Choose a server

Select a server in Billing to inspect its own plan, usage and payments. The right
card lists existing servers on usage and history tabs; Plans keeps a compact
selector in the header. **Get server** opens a separate server purchase. A server
without a plan keeps the selector so you can return to another server.

A first purchase shows **Monthly hosts** and **Prepaid PAYG**. Monthly plans
and Custom use the same purchase controls. The PAYG pricing preview has resource
tier tabs inside the resource configurator. Each tier shows its shared CPU, RAM, storage
and server allowance, together with the credit-purchase amount needed to unlock it.
Resource inputs stay intact when switching tiers. Configurations above a pool's
limits show the excess and offer a larger tier when one fits.

Choose a credit package to see its qualifying tier and estimate usage across
your configured servers at the provider's published rates. Spending credits
does not lower a tier. Purchases are not available yet; the displayed selections
do not grant access or change an existing plan.
Both purchase views end with an Enterprise contact card for larger or tailored needs.

## Coverage and usage

Monthly subscriptions include the purchased CPU, RAM and disk for the full paid
month, without another compute-credit allowance. Overview shows coverage and the
paid-through date. Usage shows recorded CPU time and resource measurements.
The server page separates actual usage from provisioned capacity; unavailable
measurements stay unknown.

Managed proxy transfer and backups are separate. Proxy-transfer exhaustion does
not end paid compute coverage. Existing metered subscriptions keep their saved
terms and credit displays; updating Openship does not convert them.

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Current plan, period, coverage and renewal controls. |
| **Usage** | Recorded resource usage over the selected period. |
| **Plans** | Review upgrades or other plans, including Custom resources. |
| **Top-ups** | Shown only for eligible saved metered subscriptions. |
| **Payments** | Invoices and cards in the server's scoped Stripe portal. |

## Change or cancel a plan

**Review change** shows the provider's amount due now, effective date and projects
that may restart. Upgrades apply after payment. Resource reductions and changes
of billing mode take effect at renewal. Review the actual quote before confirming.
If payment needs authentication, use **Complete payment**. Pending operations stay
visible and can be canceled when allowed; retries reuse the same operation.

After payment, the existing server resize worker applies the approved resources.
If projects changed after review, **Apply plan capacity** asks for an updated
restart confirmation. A resize can be retried without buying the plan again.
Disks can grow but cannot shrink in place; use Custom to retain storage while
reducing other resources, or migrate to another server.

Canceling renewal preserves the paid period. When coverage ends, compute stops
and retained disks follow the saved storage terms. Retention does not mean free
storage: billing continues until deletion, with no automatic deletion at the
minimum retention review date. Overview shows these terms and any storage amount
due. Invoices and cards stay scoped to this server, not other customers or the
provider account.

## Make room for another deployment

Apps and source builds share the managed server. A new project does not reserve
another VM or disk, and container sizes are limits within the existing host.
Image-only installs do not allocate a source builder. Inspect server usage,
stop an idle service if memory is tight, or review a larger plan. See
[Servers](/docs/dashboard/servers) for progress, logs and recovery.

## Credit warnings

Only saved metered subscriptions show low-credit notices. Monthly coverage does
not depend on an old credit balance. See
[credit warnings and recovery](/docs/guides/billing#credit-warnings-and-recovery)
for the metered flow, and the [billing API](/docs/api/billing) for the endpoints.
